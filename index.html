Privacy Policy for PWRtal

Effective date: September 26, 2026

PWRtal ("the app") is a personal media-tracking app for logging the movies, TV shows (including anime), books (including manga and graphic novels), and games you watch, read, or play. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

Data we collect

Account email. Signing in uses a passwordless "magic link" sent to your email address, handled by our backend provider, Supabase. We never see or store a password — Supabase manages the sign-in link and session token.

Your library data. Everything you log in the app — titles, status, progress, dates, your own 0–10 scores, notes, and play history — is stored under your account so it syncs across your own devices. This data is entered by you and is visible only to you (there is no public profile or shared feed in the app today).

Nothing else. PWRtal contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no tracking of any kind. We don't collect your location, contacts, device identifiers, or browsing history. The only Android permission the app requests is internet access, needed to sync your library and search for titles.

How your data is stored

Your account and library data are stored in Supabase, a hosted database provider. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third-party services used for search

When you search for a title to add to your library, the app queries one or more third-party metadata providers, depending on media type, to fetch title details and cover art:

  • TMDB (themoviedb.org) — movies and TV
  • RAWG (rawg.io) — games
  • AniList (anilist.co) and Jikan / MyAnimeList (myanimelist.net) — anime and manga
  • Kitsu (kitsu.io) — anime and manga
  • Open Library (openlibrary.org) and Google Books (books.google.com) — books, and whether a book is sold on Google Play Books in your country
  • MangaUpdates (mangaupdates.com) and WEBTOON (webtoons.com) — the latest chapter of a running manga or webtoon, when you refresh it, and a manga's English publishers when you open it. These requests go through the app's own server, so the service sees only the title, not your device.
  • Audible (audible.com and its regional stores) — whether a book is on Audible in your country, when you open it. This goes through the app's own server, so Audible sees only the title, author and store, not your device.

Only the search text you type, or the title and author being looked up (and, for availability, your country), is sent to these services — no account or personal information is included in these requests. Each provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle that request.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any entry in your library at any time from within the app.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app: Profile → Delete account.

This removes your entries, every play and rating, your settings, any feedback you have sent, and the account itself. It is immediate and permanent — there is no recovery period and no backup to restore from, so please export your data first if you want to keep a copy (Profile → Data → Export).

You may sign up again afterwards with the same email address; it starts a new, empty account with no connection to the deleted one.

If you cannot sign in and so cannot reach that screen, see Delete your PWRtal account.

Children's privacy

PWRtal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
