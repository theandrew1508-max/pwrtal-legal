Delete your PWRtal account

PWRtal lets you delete your account, and everything stored with it, at any time. You do not need to contact anyone to do it.

From inside the app

  1. Open PWRtal and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to the bottom, to Danger zone.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Type your email address to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

What is deleted

Everything, permanently:

  • Every entry in your library, with all of its plays, ratings, dates, progress and notes
  • Your watchlist
  • All of your settings and preferences
  • Any feedback you have sent through the app
  • Your account itself, and the email address associated with it

Nothing is retained after deletion, and no data is kept for any period afterwards. It cannot be undone, and there is no backup to restore from — so if you want a copy of your library, export it first from Profile → Data → Export, which gives you a spreadsheet of everything you have logged.

You are welcome to sign up again later with the same email address. It starts a new, empty account with no connection to the deleted one.

If you cannot sign in

If you have lost access to your account and cannot reach the screen above, email theandrew1508@googlemail.com from the email address the account uses, asking for the account to be deleted. Requests are handled manually and confirmed by reply.
